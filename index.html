Data and limitations
  • Data is saved in this browser's local storage; it is not synced to a server.
  • Valuations are indicative rule-based estimates, not certified appraisals.
  • Battery SOH requires supported BMS diagnostics or independent testing.
  • No live OEM, VAHAN, lender, auction or market transaction feeds are connected.
  • Authentication, role permissions, cloud database, payment gateway and production security are not yet implemented.
